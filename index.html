The essay so far

Neither side could win, but a stalemate does not negotiate itself: leaders had to choose to act on it, and in 1990 De Klerk and Mandela did. Between 1990 and 1992 both sides gave up things nobody could make them give up, which is what political will looks like in practice. But violence repeatedly did what it was meant to do and stopped the talks, so will alone cannot explain the outcome.

Sentence three is the one that usually goes missing. It concedes. A question that asks to what extent is inviting you to say how far, and you cannot say how far if you only ever argue one way.
